Physical design

Tiqora has a clean layout at 3x2, the size info.yaml submits. The Tiny Tapeout gds, pre-flight and gate-level workflows pass (GitHub run 40213473443, LibreLane 3.0.14). A local run of the same configuration routes with 0 DRC, 0 LVS and 0 antenna errors and meets timing at the typical corner; it misses setup at the slowest corner by 0.75 ns measured. No chip exists yet.

Three different areas

They are easy to mix up, and the numbers below use each in exactly one sense.

Die area

114,858 µm²: the 3x2 tile, 508.76 × 225.76 µm, from Tiny Tapeout's template tt_block_3x2_pg.def. Fixed by the tile count.

Core area

The part of the die where cells may be placed, inside the power ring and the pin keep-outs. 110,874 µm² measured, 61% filled with cells after placement and timing repair (measured, local LibreLane 3.0.14 run of src/config.json, 2026-10-26).

Summed cell area

50,880 µm² measured: every standard cell Yosys 0.33 maps the flat design to, added up (make area, tt_025C_1v80), using the cells src/synth_exclude.cells leaves. Before placement, so with no timing-repair buffers, taps or fill. 44% of the die.

Where the cells go

Each block's cells, synthesized one module at a time and drawn to scale inside the 3x2 die outline. Positions mean nothing: the placer would spread these cells over the whole core, mixed together.

How it got to fit

At 2x2, placement failed with DPL-0036. Two things were wrong. LibreLane's default cell list (the PDK's no_synth.cells) bans the drive-strength-1 cells, so synthesis used larger cells and the design came out at 59,797 µm², 80% of the 2x2 die; and even at the smaller size, 2x2 left too little room to route. src/synth_exclude.cells allows the drive-strength-1 cells (the cells with known layout problems stay banned through the PDK's separate list), and the tile count went to 3x2 at placement density 50. With both, the gds, precheck and gl_test workflows pass measured (GitHub run 40213473443).

Routing and timing

Locally, detailed routing first stalled on its last few shorts for hours: global routing held back 30% of the tracks and ended with 58 overflowing regions. With GRT_ADJUSTMENT at 0.15 global routing has no overflow and detailed routing finishes with 0 violations in about half an hour. Smaller clock-tree clusters (CTS_SINK_CLUSTERING_SIZE 10) and timing repair after global routing then took the worst slow-corner slack from −1.10 ns to −0.75 ns measured (measured, local LibreLane 3.0.14 run of src/config.json, 2026-10-26).

The same run still reports slow-corner slew warnings on about 1,550 pins; the resizer does not clear them after the final routing.
