Physical design

There is no Tiqora layout yet. At the 2x2 tile size info.yaml submits, LibreLane 3.0.14 stops in detailed placement with DPL-0036, both on GitHub and locally. So this page has no layout image, no utilisation map, no routed timing and no power map: none of them exists to show.

Three different areas

They are easy to mix up, and the numbers below use each in exactly one sense.

Die area

75,602 µm²: the 2x2 tile, 334.88 × 225.76 µm, from Tiny Tapeout's template tt_block_2x2_pg.def. Fixed by the tile count.

Core area

The part of the die where cells may be placed, inside the power ring and the pin keep-outs. Only a layout run reports it; for Tiqora none has reached that point at 2x2.

Summed cell area

60,750 µm² measured: every standard cell Yosys 0.33 maps the flat design to, added up (make area, tt_025C_1v80), using only the cells the Tiny Tapeout flow allows. Before placement, so with no timing-repair buffers, taps or fill. 80% of the die.

Where the cells go

Each block's cells, synthesized one module at a time and drawn to scale inside the 2x2 die outline. Positions mean nothing: the placer would spread these cells over the whole core, mixed together.

Why it does not fit yet

Placement needs room to spread cells, add timing-repair buffers and route wires. Cell area at 80% of the die leaves too little: DPL-0036 means the detailed placer could not legalise every cell. An experimental copy at 3x2 got through placement but did not converge in routing (10,796 violations at pass 8). For comparison, Tiqora-Serial's experimental 2x2 copy, at 33% of its die in synthesized cells, hardened clean.

The way forward is the owner's decision: a larger tile set, or less logic. Either way, the next measurement is a clean LibreLane run, then the Tiny Tapeout pre-flight, then signoff timing.

The images in docs/images/layout_blocks.png and layout_zoom.png are illustrations, not a placed design, and are not used on this page.
