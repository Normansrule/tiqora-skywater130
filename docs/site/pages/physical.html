Physical design

Tiqora has a clean layout at 3x2, the size info.yaml submits. The Tiny Tapeout gds, pre-flight and gate-level workflows pass (GitHub run 40213473443, LibreLane 3.0.14). Its routed timing reports have not been read yet: the workflow does not fail on timing, and its log files could not be downloaded where this page was written. No chip exists yet.

Three different areas

They are easy to mix up, and the numbers below use each in exactly one sense.

Die area

114,858 µm²: the 3x2 tile, 508.76 × 225.76 µm, from Tiny Tapeout's template tt_block_3x2_pg.def. Fixed by the tile count.

Core area

The part of the die where cells may be placed, inside the power ring and the pin keep-outs. Only a layout run reports it; the numbers from the passing run are in its log files, not yet read.

Summed cell area

50,880 µm² measured: every standard cell Yosys 0.33 maps the flat design to, added up (make area, tt_025C_1v80), using the cells src/synth_exclude.cells leaves. Before placement, so with no timing-repair buffers, taps or fill. 44% of the die.

Where the cells go

Each block's cells, synthesized one module at a time and drawn to scale inside the 3x2 die outline. Positions mean nothing: the placer would spread these cells over the whole core, mixed together.

How it got to fit

At 2x2, placement failed with DPL-0036. Two things were wrong. LibreLane's default cell list (the PDK's no_synth.cells) bans the drive-strength-1 cells, so synthesis used larger cells and the design came out at 59,797 µm², 80% of the 2x2 die; and even at the smaller size, 2x2 left too little room to route. src/synth_exclude.cells allows the drive-strength-1 cells (the cells with known layout problems stay banned through the PDK's separate list), and the tile count went to 3x2 at placement density 50. With both, the gds, precheck and gl_test workflows pass measured (GitHub run 40213473443).

Still to read from that run: the core area, the utilisation after placement, and the setup and hold slack at each corner. Tiqora-Serial's 1x2 layout misses setup at the slow corner by 1.30 ns, so Tiqora's slow-corner number matters before a submission.
