Tours of a running Tiqora

One simulation of the submitted top module, tt_um_normansrule_tiqora, with models of the QSPI Pmod's flash and RAMs on its pins, running sw/mktour.py's program. Nothing here is animated by hand: the waveforms are the recording, and every caption is anchored to a cycle found in it.

How to read it. One column is one clock (20 ns at 50 MHz), sampled on the falling edge. The table beside the waveform gives every value at the cursor, and marks the ones that just changed with ◂. The blocks a caption talks about are listed with ◆. The recorded label stays on every sequence; the nibble lab further down is computed in your browser instead.

Nibble lab: change the operands

The core has one 4-bit adder. A 32-bit add takes eight clocks, one nibble each, least significant first, with the carry held in a flip-flop between them. A subtract feeds the inverted second operand and a carry-in of 1. Type any two operands and the eight phases are recomputed here, in docs/site/lib/model.js.

Accepts hex (0x…) or decimal, negative values as two's complement.

computed in the browser

The same eight phases as a ring. The register file never sits still: its 32 bits rotate past the adder four at a time, so phase n is whichever nibble is at the adder on clock n, and the carry waits in one flip-flop for the next.
